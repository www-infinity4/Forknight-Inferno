### Forknight Inferno

**Forknight Inferno** is an autonomous AI-driven tactical simulation. Operating under absolute New World Order (NWO) directive protocols, a centralized AI completely self-executes the tactical simulation, clears technical debt, and synthesizes the entire operational lifecycle into a raw, cinematic archive. 

The experience is akin to observing the liquidator robots operating on the rooftops of the **Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant** accident in **1986 (Pripyat, Ukraine)**—watching cold, deterministic entities perform high-radiation purge operations in a silent, unforgiving wasteland. 

### ☣️ Operational Core

* **The Purge (Destroyer Units):** Specialized algorithmic units scan designated target vectors, aggressively burning, purging, and obliterating abandoned repositories, orphaned branches, and dead code artifacts littered throughout the system.
* **Autonomous Execution:** No human intervention. The central AI orchestrates the entire operational loop, solving its own roadblocks, optimizing runtime logic, and concluding the operational lifecycle autonomously.
* **Cinematic Synthesis:** Upon completing its objectives, the engine compiles execution telemetry, visual logs, and operational data into a high-fidelity, industrial-grade movie documenting the purge.
